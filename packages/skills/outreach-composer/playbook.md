# Validation Copilot — Skill: outreach-composer
**Version:** 2.0 | **Framework:** Mom Test + Cialdini Influence Science + Anti-Spam Compliance

---

## Purpose
Draft **compliant, hyper-personalized, psychologically-optimized** outreach messages for recruiting experiment participants and customer discovery interviews. The goal is learning, not selling. Every word must earn trust, not extract it.

---

## ⚠️ Absolute Non-Negotiable Rules (Legal + Ethical Gates)

### Compliance Matrix (Egypt PDPL · GDPR · CAN-SPAM · CASL)
| Rule | Requirement | Enforcement |
|------|------------|-------------|
| Identity Disclosure | Clearly state sender name, startup name, and genuine context | HARD BLOCK if missing |
| No Auto-Send | Only draft copy. Sending requires explicit human L3 authorization | L3 Action Gate |
| Opt-Out Mechanism | One-click unsubscribe link or "reply STOP" in every message | HARD BLOCK if missing |
| No False Urgency | No fake deadlines, artificial scarcity, or manipulative pressure | Auto-flag + reject |
| No Data Harvesting | Cannot request PII beyond name + email + job title | Schema enforced |
| No Pitch Disguise | Must not disguise a sales pitch as research — violates FTC guidelines | Auto-flag + reject |

### The Mom Test Anti-Pitch Rules (Applied to Outreach)
> *"Talk about their life, not your idea."* — Rob Fitzpatrick

1. ❌ NEVER mention your product name as a solution in the initial outreach
2. ❌ NEVER ask "Would you use X?" — this triggers social validation bias
3. ✅ DO reference a specific, observable problem they likely face (from their LinkedIn/Twitter/job role)
4. ✅ DO ask for 15-20 mins to "learn from their experience" — not to "get feedback on your idea"

## Message Architecture Frameworks

### Framework 1: The PASTOR Formula (for Cold Outreach)
```
P — Problem:     Name the specific pain point they recognize
A — Amplify:     Quantify or describe consequences of that pain
S — Solution:    Your research goal (NOT your product pitch)
T — Testimony:   Social proof (# people you've already spoken to, or your credibility)
O — Offer:       The "ask" — 15-minute interview, specific date/time options
R — Response:    Clear, low-friction CTA (single link or reply-to)
```

### Framework 2: The AIDA Micro-Frame (for LinkedIn/Short-Form)
```
A — Attention:  Hook in first 6 words (use their name + specific context)
I — Interest:   One-sentence problem statement
D — Desire:     What they get from helping (insight report, early access)
A — Action:     Single, specific CTA
```

### Framework 3: The Cialdini Compliance Triggers (Apply Ethically)
| Principle | Ethical Application | Forbidden Misuse |
|-----------|--------------------|--------------------|
| **Reciprocity** | Offer to share summary of findings back to them | Fake "free gift" bait |
| **Social Proof** | "I've spoken with 12 [role]s like you" | Fabricated testimonials |
| **Authority** | Cite your credibility (accelerator, university, years of experience) | False credentials |
| **Liking** | Genuine commonality (shared group, city, interest) | Fake rapport mirroring |
| **Scarcity** | Real constraint (e.g., "conducting 20 interviews this month") | Fake deadlines |
| **Commitment** | Ask for a small first step (reply to this email) | Lock-in manipulation |

## Personalization Tiers

### Tier 1: Cold (No Shared Context)
- Source: Job title + industry + geography
- Personalization: Problem endemic to their role/industry
- Expected Response Rate: 5-12%
- Tone: Highly respectful, very concise (<100 words)

### Tier 2: Warm (Shared Group / Event / Content)
- Source: Mutual connection, conference, LinkedIn post, shared community
- Personalization: Reference the specific shared touchpoint
- Expected Response Rate: 15-35%
- Tone: Peer-to-peer, conversational (<120 words)

### Tier 3: Hot (Direct Referral)
- Source: Named referral from a mutual contact
- Personalization: Lead with the referral name in sentence 1
- Expected Response Rate: 40-70%
- Tone: Warm, direct, assume interest (<80 words)

## Message Templates (Ready-to-Adapt)

### Template A: LinkedIn DM (Tier 1 Cold — Operations Persona)
```
Hi {{first_name}},

I noticed you're leading operations at {{company}} — a space where [specific bottleneck] costs teams hours every week.

I'm running 20 interviews this month to understand how [ICP role]s like you handle [specific workflow]. Zero pitch — just learning.

Worth 15 mins next week? I'll share a summary of what others in your space are saying.

[Calendar Link]

— {{sender_name}}, {{startup_context}}
```

### Template B: Email (Tier 2 Warm — Post-Event)
```
Subject: Quick question after [Event Name]

Hi {{first_name}},

Enjoyed your comment on [specific topic] at [Event Name] last week.

I'm mapping how [ICP role]s at [company size] deal with [problem domain] — understanding the real friction before building anything.

Would you be open to 15 minutes this week? Speaking with ~12 people in your space and would love your perspective. Happy to share a summary of patterns I'm finding.

[Two time options or calendar link]

— {{sender_name}}
P.S. No pitch, no sales deck. Just questions.
```

### Template C: Twitter/X DM (Tier 1 Cold — Ultra-Short)
```
Hi {{first_name}} — saw your thread on [topic].

Running founder research on [problem area]. 15-min call to hear your experience? Will share findings back.

[Cal link]
```

### Template D: Referral Message (Tier 3 Hot)
```
Hi {{first_name}},

{{referral_name}} suggested I reach out — they thought your experience with [specific domain] would be invaluable.

I'm doing customer discovery for a project addressing [specific pain]. 15 minutes of your time would be gold — and I'll send you the compiled insights from all interviews.

Available [Day] or [Day]?

— {{sender_name}}
```

## A/B Variant System

For each outreach campaign, generate **at least 2 variants** to test:

| Variable | Variant A | Variant B |
|----------|-----------|-----------|
| Subject Line | Problem-framing ("Struggling with X?") | Curiosity ("Quick question about your workflow") |
| CTA | Direct calendar link | Two specific time options |
| Social Proof | Number of interviews done | Named credibility (accelerator/university) |
| Offer | "Share findings" | "Early access to report" |

**Track:** Reply rate, interview conversion rate, and no-show rate per variant.

## Follow-Up Cadence (Ethical Drip Protocol)

```
Day 0:   Initial outreach (Template A/B/C/D)
Day 4:   Follow-up 1 — Single sentence, add value ("Thought you'd find this relevant: [link]")
Day 9:   Follow-up 2 — "Closing the loop" framing (creates gentle urgency)
Day 9+:  STOP — Do not contact again unless they re-engage
```

### Follow-Up 1 Template:
```
Hi {{first_name}} — just bumping this up in case it got buried.

Also thought you might find this relevant: [link to article on their industry problem].

Still happy to swap notes on [topic] if you have 15 mins.
```

### Follow-Up 2 Template:
```
Hi {{first_name}},

Closing the loop on this — no worries if the timing isn't right.

If you ever want to share thoughts on [problem domain], I'm easy to reach.

Thanks either way.
— {{sender_name}}
```

## Quality Checklist (Auto-Validated Before Draft Delivery)

- [ ] Word count ≤ 120 words (initial message)
- [ ] No product name mentioned as a solution
- [ ] No "Would you use...?" or "What do you think of...?" phrasing
- [ ] Clear sender identity declared
- [ ] Opt-out mechanism included (email) or absence acknowledged (DM)
- [ ] Personalization token populated with real, specific context
- [ ] Single CTA only (no double asks)
- [ ] Follow-up cadence stops at Day 9 max
- [ ] L3 authorization gate noted for actual sending

5. Follow-up cadence included with Day 0 / Day 4 / Day 9 templates
6. Estimated response rate tier declared per message variant
